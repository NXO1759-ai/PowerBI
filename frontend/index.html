      About This Terminal

      Research Terminal is an independent equity-research project built and maintained by Chinedu Okeke.

      Fundamentals are pulled straight from SEC EDGAR XBRL filings into a Postgres warehouse, market activity comes from live exchange feeds, and every covered company gets a filing-grounded analyst brief generated on demand. The same database powers the Power BI semantic model behind this site.

      More dashboards are on the way: financial statement analysis, private credit reports, and strategy analysis.
